Bio

Before joining SCU, I was a Research Scholar at UC Berkeley for two years, working with Professor Masayoshi Tomizuka. I previously held an Assistant Professor position at Hacettepe University and spent a decade as a Senior Control System Design Engineer at Aselsan.

I received my Ph.D. and M.Sc. from TOBB University of Economics and Technology in 2019 and 2015, and my B.Sc. from Istanbul Technical University in 2010. I received the IEEE Turkey Ph.D. Thesis Award in 2020. I am a Senior Member of the IEEE and an Associate Editor of TIMC, Measurement and Control, and the Turkish Journal of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Uncertainty-aware control: disturbance estimation, stable inversion, and nonlinear output-feedback control. I also study Koopman-Galerkin methods based on infinite-dimensional operator representations.
  • Soft robotics: modeling, design, and control of soft actuators and robotic systems.
  • Learning and data-driven modeling: system identification and methods for learning multiple functions and datasets within a common model, including switched neural networks and simultaneous learning.

Application areas include soft robots, biomimetic systems, gimbal stabilization, motion-control platforms, UAV flight control, and autonomous multi-agent systems.
